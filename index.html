Jungwon Kong

Ph.D. Candidate, Technology Management, Economics and Policy Program, Seoul National University

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Technology Management, Economics and Policy Program at Seoul National University. My research explores technology management and innovation in creative industries, with a particular focus on how artificial intelligence reshapes creativity and knowledge flows in the music industry. Methodologically, I draw on computational social science, econometric analysis, patent analysis, and natural language processing.
